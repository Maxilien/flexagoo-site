1. What FlexaGo is

FlexaGo is a peer-to-peer marketplace. People who need an item delivered (“Senders”) post a delivery, and people who are already traveling along that route (“Travelers”) can choose to carry it. Senders and Travelers are “members.” The person receiving an item is the “receiver.”

  • FlexaGo is not a carrier, courier, freight forwarder or moving company. We do not pick up, hold, transport or deliver items ourselves, and we never take ownership of any item.
  • Each delivery is an agreement directly between the Sender and the Traveler. FlexaGo provides the technology to find each other, verify identities, communicate, track progress and handle payment.
  • Travelers are independent. They are not FlexaGo's employees, agents or partners. They decide whether, when and which deliveries to accept, and how to travel.
  • FlexaGo is launching first in Texas. Some features, delivery types or areas may not be available everywhere.

7. How a delivery works

  1. Post: the Sender describes the item, pickup and drop-off, and pays the price shown.
  2. Accept: a verified Traveler on that route accepts. The Sender and Traveler can chat in the app.
  3. Pick up: the Traveler meets the Sender, checks the item and enters the Sender's pickup code. A wrong code too many times locks the pickup for a short time to protect the Sender.
  4. Deliver: the Traveler hands the item to the receiver and records proof of delivery.
  5. Confirm and pay: once delivery is confirmed, the Traveler's payout is released and both members can rate each other.

Delivery times shown in the app are estimates. Weather, traffic, travel delays and other things outside anyone's control can cause changes.

8. Prices, payments and fees

  • Price: FlexaGo calculates each delivery's price from distance, delivery type, size, weight, item type, declared value and any protection option. The full price is shown before you pay.
