About FlexaGo

FlexaGo is a people‑powered way to move things from one place to another. We connect senders with travelers already heading that way — simple, fast, and built around real people helping real people.

How FlexaGo Works

1. Sender Creates a Request

Describe your item, pickup, and drop‑off.

2. Traveler Accepts & Delivers

Verified travelers pick up your item and deliver it along their route.

3. Delivery Completed

Traveler hands the package to the receiver — simple and secure.
