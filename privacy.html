Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

FlexaGo is a peer-to-peer delivery marketplace run by Flexago LLC (“FlexaGo”, “we”, “us”, “our”). We connect people who need something delivered (“Senders”) with verified people who are already traveling that way (“Travelers”). This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the FlexaGo app, flexagoo.com, app.flexagoo.com and our text messages, emails and support (together, the “Services”).

The short version

  • We collect what a safe delivery marketplace needs: your account details, identity and background check results, delivery details, payments and messages.
  • Senders and Travelers see only what they need to complete a delivery, such as name, photo, rating, and pickup and drop-off details. Phone numbers are shared only after a Traveler accepts.
  • We use trusted service providers for payments, identity checks, background checks, text messages and hosting.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we never share your phone number or text-message consent for anyone's marketing.
  • You can see, correct or delete your information. Some records must be kept for legal, tax and safety reasons.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile phone number, date of birth, password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), role (Sender or Traveler), profile photo and language preference.
  • Identity verification: a photo of your government ID and a selfie, used to confirm you are who you say you are (see section 6).
  • Background check information: the details our background check provider needs, which you give directly to that provider, and the result it returns to us.
  • Delivery details (Senders): pickup and drop-off addresses, item description, category, size, weight, declared value, photos of the item, receiver's name and phone number, delivery instructions and any protection option you choose.
  • Trip details (Travelers): your route, travel dates, vehicle or travel mode and the deliveries you accept.
  • Proof of delivery: pickup codes, photos taken at pickup and drop-off, and confirmation times.
  • Messages and support: in-app chat between a Sender and a Traveler, support tickets, safety reports, ratings and reviews.
  • Payments and payouts: Senders' payment cards and Travelers' payout accounts are entered directly with our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store full card or bank account numbers; we receive a token, the card brand, the last four digits and payment status.
  • Rewards: referral codes you use or share, promo codes you apply, and credits or bonuses you earn.
  • Waitlist: if you join our waitlist on flexagoo.com, your email address and city.

Information about receivers and other people

When a Sender enters a receiver's name, phone number and address, we use them only to complete that delivery, for example to let the Traveler find the receiver and to send delivery updates. Senders must have the receiver's permission to share these details with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, device type, operating system, app version, browser type, pages and screens viewed, actions taken, dates and times, and crash and error logs.
  • Location: see section 7.

Information from others

  • Identity verification results from Stripe Identity.
  • Background check results from Checkr.
  • Payment, payout and fraud signals from Stripe.
  • Ratings, reviews and reports other members submit about you.
  • The referral code of the member who invited you, if any.

2. How we use information

  • Run the marketplace: create your account, match deliveries with Travelers on the same route, calculate prices, show pickup and drop-off details, and track delivery status.
  • Keep members safe: verify identity and age, run background checks, check pickup codes, prevent fraud and misuse, investigate reports, and block or remove accounts that break our rules.
  • Handle money: charge Senders, pay Travelers, issue refunds and cancellation payments, apply promo codes and credits, and pay rewards.
  • Communicate with you: send verification codes, delivery updates, security alerts, receipts and support replies.
  • Support and disputes: answer questions, review protection requests and resolve disagreements between members.
  • Improve the Services: understand how the Services are used, fix problems and build new features.
  • Legal duties: keep tax and financial records, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use automated decisions alone to permanently remove an account. A person at FlexaGo reviews background check results that are not “clear” and serious safety reports before final action is taken.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share information only:

  • With other members, as described in section 3.
  • With service providers who work for us under contract and may use the information only to provide their service:
    • Stripe: payments, payouts, fraud prevention and identity verification (Stripe Identity)
    • Checkr: background checks
    • Twilio and mobile carriers: text messages and phone verification
    • Cloudinary: storing item, profile and proof-of-delivery photos
    • MongoDB Atlas: our database
    • Render: hosting our servers
    • Google Cloud Translation: translating text when you choose to translate it
    • Our email provider: sending account and delivery emails
  • For safety and the law: when we believe in good faith it is needed to comply with law or legal process, protect someone's safety, investigate fraud, or enforce our Terms. This may include sharing with law enforcement or with a member involved in a reported incident.
  • For protection requests and disputes: with the other member on the delivery, and with any insurer or claims administrator that helps us review a request.
  • Business changes: if FlexaGo is involved in a merger, financing, acquisition or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that deal, under this policy.
  • With your permission, for any other purpose you agree to.

We may share information that is combined or de-identified so that it no longer identifies you, for example total deliveries by city.

5. Phone numbers and text messages

Your mobile number is used for:

  • Account creation and phone verification
  • Login security and verification codes
  • Security alerts
  • Delivery updates and confirmations
  • Support and troubleshooting

Mobile information disclosure

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This includes phone numbers, text-message opt-in data and consent, message content and delivery information. Mobile information may be shared only with our text-message providers (such as Twilio) and mobile carriers, solely to deliver messages that are part of the FlexaGo service.

Consent and opting out

By entering your mobile number and tapping “Send code” during signup, you agree to receive text messages from FlexaGo for account verification, login security, delivery notifications and important account updates. Message frequency varies with your account activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Opting out of non-essential texts will not close your account, but we may still need to send one-time verification codes when you sign in. More details are in the Text Message Terms.

6. Identity, biometrics and background checks

Identity verification

Every member verifies their identity before sending or carrying deliveries. Verification is done by Stripe Identity: you photograph your government ID and take a selfie, and Stripe compares the two to confirm they match. This comparison may use facial geometry, which some laws (including Texas law) treat as a biometric identifier.

  • By starting verification, you consent to Stripe capturing and comparing these images for identity verification and fraud prevention.
  • FlexaGo does not receive or store your facial geometry. We receive the verification result and the verified name and date of birth, which we compare with your account.
  • Stripe keeps verification data under its own privacy policy and retention rules. We do not sell, lease or disclose biometric identifiers.

Background checks

Members must pass a background check before using the marketplace. Checks are run by Checkr, a consumer reporting agency, only after you give your authorization on Checkr's own form. You give sensitive details (such as your Social Security number) directly to Checkr; FlexaGo does not collect or store them. We receive the result status (for example “clear” or “consider”) and only the report details needed to make a decision.

If we may decide not to allow you on FlexaGo because of something in your report, we follow the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act: we send you a copy of the report and a summary of your rights first, give you time to dispute anything inaccurate with Checkr, and only then make a final decision.

7. Location information

  • Addresses you enter are used to match routes, calculate distance and price, and guide pickup and drop-off.
  • Device location (if you allow it) is used to fill in your location and show deliveries near you. If we offer live delivery tracking, a Traveler's location will be shared with the Sender only while that Traveler is carrying the Sender's delivery, never at other times.
  • Approximate location from your IP address may be used for security, fraud prevention and to show your city on our website.

You can turn off location access in your device settings. Some features may not work as well without it.

8. Website, cookies and similar tools

  • Our website and app use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember choices such as language. These are needed for the Services to work.
  • To show your city in our website footer, your browser asks a location lookup service (ipapi.co or ipwho.is) for an approximate location based on your IP address. We do not store this.
  • Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font is downloaded.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers. If we add analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy first.

10. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we have about you, and get a copy in a portable format.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Delete your information. You can delete your account in the app under Profile, or email us.
  • Opt out of text messages (reply STOP) and of non-essential emails (use the link in the email).

Texas residents have these rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, and residents of other states (such as California, Colorado, Virginia and Connecticut) may have similar rights. We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising or use it for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects, so there is nothing to opt out of there.

To make a request, email support@flexagoo.com from the email on your account. We will confirm your identity before acting, and reply within 45 days (we may extend this once by another 45 days when reasonably necessary, and will tell you if we do). If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with “Appeal” in the subject line; we will respond within 60 days. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Texas Attorney General at texasattorneygeneral.gov. You may use an authorized agent, and we will never treat you differently for using your rights.

11. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls that limit staff access to what their job needs, rate limits against guessing attacks, and providers that hold recognized security certifications. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

12. Children

You must be at least 18 years old to use FlexaGo. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. International deliveries and data transfers

FlexaGo is based in the United States and our providers store information in the United States. For deliveries that cross borders, some delivery details may need to be shared with customs or other authorities, and with the Traveler in the destination country. If you use the Services from outside the United States, you understand your information will be processed in the United States.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services change. We will change the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
